# Privacy Policy — HiveFit

**Last updated:** April 2026

## What is HiveFit?

HiveFit is a personal fitness tracking app for Android. It runs entirely on your device — all workout data, body metrics, and progress history are stored locally in a private database on your phone. No data is sent to external servers by default.

## Data stored on your device

HiveFit stores the following data **locally on your device only**:

- Workout sessions, exercises, sets, reps, and weights
- Personal records (PRs) and 1RM estimates
- Body metrics (weight, body fat %, BMR, BMI) synced from Health Connect
- Daily health data (steps, active calories, resting heart rate, sleep) synced from Health Connect
- User profiles (name, avatar colour)
- App preferences

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable the optional OpenHive integration (see below).

## Android Health Connect

HiveFit requests access to Android Health Connect to read:

- **Weight** — to track your body weight over time
- **Body Fat** — to track body composition
- **Steps, Active Calories, Distance** — to display daily activity on the Home screen
- **Resting Heart Rate, Sleep** — to display daily health summary

Health Connect data is read into the app for display purposes only. It is stored in your local app database and is never transmitted to any external service.

You can revoke Health Connect permissions at any time via **Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → HiveFit**.

## Optional: OpenHive integration

HiveFit includes an optional connection to an **OpenHive server**. OpenHive is self-hosted software — you run it on your own hardware or private network. If you choose to connect:

- Finished workouts are pushed to **your own OpenHive server** for AI-assisted logging and review
- Workout plans generated by your OpenHive AI assistant can be imported into the app
- No data is sent to any third-party server — only to the OpenHive server address you provide

You can disconnect OpenHive at any time via **More → Profiles → your profile → Disconnect OpenHive**.

## Data collection by the app developer

HiveFit does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app contains:

- No analytics SDKs
- No advertising SDKs
- No crash reporting services
- No third-party tracking of any kind

## Data deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. To delete it, uninstall the app or clear app data via **Android Settings → Apps → HiveFit → Storage → Clear Data**.
